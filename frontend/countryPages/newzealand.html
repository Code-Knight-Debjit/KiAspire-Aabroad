Overview

Eight universities. No overcrowded lecture halls.

New Zealand runs a deliberately small, all-public university system — eight universities in total, every one of them internationally ranked. That scale means smaller class sizes and closer contact with faculty than students often get at much larger systems, without sacrificing research depth, particularly at the University of Auckland, the country's flagship institution.

  • All eight New Zealand universities are internationally ranked.
  • University of Auckland — New Zealand's top-ranked, globally top-100 institution.
  • A genuine post-study work visa pathway for graduates.

What You'll Need

Admission requirements at a glance.

Academic Records

  • Academic transcripts and certificates
  • Statement of purpose
  • Letters of recommendation, where required
  • Portfolio, for design and creative programs

English Proficiency

  • IELTS Academic, typically 6.0–6.5 overall
  • PTE Academic accepted by most universities
  • TOEFL iBT accepted by most universities

Financial Documentation

  • Proof of funds for tuition and living costs
  • Sponsor's bank statements, if applicable
  • Confirmation of accommodation costs

Visa & Supporting Documents

  • Valid passport
  • Offer of place from a New Zealand institution
  • Fee Paying Student visa application (Immigration New Zealand)
  • Medical and character requirements, where applicable
